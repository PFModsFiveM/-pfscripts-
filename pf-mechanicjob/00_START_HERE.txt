                           FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MECHANICAL COMPONENTS:
- 30+ part types with realistic specifications
- Engine blocks, internals, turbos, superchargers
- Intercoolers, intake/exhaust systems
- Fuel systems, cooling, oil
- Clutches, gearboxes, differentials
- Tire compounds (street to slick)

REALISTIC POWER DELIVERY:
- No flat multipliers - torque curves by RPM
- Stock: flat at 1.0x
- Turbo: peaks mid-high RPM
- Supercharger: peaks low RPM
- 5 total curves for different builds

FUEL CONSUMPTION:
- Formula: 1.0 + (hp ^ 1.3)
- Stock (150 HP): 1.0x = 45 mpg baseline
- Daily driver (250 HP): 2.0x = 22.5 mpg
- High-perf (500 HP): 3.2x = 14 mpg
- Extreme (1000 HP): 6.1x = 7.4 mpg

HEAT SYSTEM:
- 75°C: Performance reduction starts
- 80°C: Noticeable power loss
- 90°C: Limp mode (40% power, 4000 RPM cap)
- 92°C: Engine damage begins
- 100°C: Forced shutdown

DRIVETRAIN STRESS:
- Launch detection (RPM > 4000, speed < 10 mph, throttle > 80%)
- Clutch wear: 5.0x multiplier on launch
- Wheelpspin tracking with penalty system
- Torque rating enforcement
- Gear slip when over-limit

TIRE PHYSICS:
- Street (0.85 grip): Long-lasting
- Sport (1.0 grip): Balanced
- Semi-slick (1.25 grip): Fast wear
- Slick (1.50 grip): Track-only max grip
- Wheelspin causes wear acceleration

VALIDATION RULES:
✓ Turbo Stage 2 requires intercooler
✓ High boost requires forged internals
✓ Stage 2+ requires fuel upgrade
✓ Cooling limits enforced
✓ Drivetrain capability checked

CONSEQUENCES:
- 1000 HP = 6.1x fuel, 3.5x maintenance, 2-10% failure risk
- 250 HP = 2.0x fuel, 1.3x maintenance, reliable
- Stock = 1.0x fuel, no service needed
